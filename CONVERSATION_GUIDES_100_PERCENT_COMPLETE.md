### Core Metadata (8 fields)
- ✅ **title**: Full descriptive title with product, study, and date
- ✅ **author**: Researcher/moderator names with team attribution

- ✅ **last_modified**: Updated timestamp
- ✅ **team**: Full team name with proper capitalization
- ✅ **product**: Specific product/feature name
- ✅ **product_area**: Authenticated or unauthenticated
- ✅ **portfolio**: Health, Benefits, or Digital Experience

### Research Context (9 fields)
- ✅ **study_name**: Official research study name
- ✅ **research_phase**: Discovery, usability, UAT, etc.
- ✅ **research_type**: Moderated interviews, usability testing, etc.
- ✅ **research_method**: Specific methodology details
- ✅ **session_format**: Remote/in-person moderated sessions
- ✅ **session_duration**: Accurate time estimates (15-75 minutes)
- ✅ **participant_type**: Clear participant criteria
- ✅ **related_research_plan**: Links to companion documents
- ✅ **related_prototype**: URLs or environment specifications

### Logistics (5-7 fields)
- ✅ **recording_required**: Boolean specification
- ✅ **consent_for_clips**: Video clip consent status
- ✅ **observer_support**: Observer participation
- ✅ **screen_sharing**: When applicable
- ✅ **live_data**: For production testing
- ✅ **assistive_tech_support**: For accessibility studies
- ✅ **informed_consent**: For onsite research

### Discoverability
- ✅ **tags**: 15-30 comprehensive tags per file including:
  - Research methodology tags
  - Product/feature tags
  - Technology/platform tags
  - Benefit area tags
  - Special characteristics (accessibility, mobile, etc.)
- ✅ **description**: 2-3 sentence summary explaining research purpose, methods, and goals
