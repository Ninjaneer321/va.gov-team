## Analysis
[Resources and support ratings spreadsheet](https://dvagov-my.sharepoint.com/:x:/r/personal/randi_hecht_va_gov/Documents/Documents/Analytics/Resources%20and%20support%20ratings%20tracking%20March%202026.xlsx?d=wf194bda3553d45cc9d590e296c3cd180&csf=1&web=1&e=A7BLjr)
- We came close but didn't meet our goal this month of 75% good rating for resources and support pages, with an average rating this month of 74%. 
- Performance isn't as high when broken down to individual pages:
  - 32% of pages are scoring at 90% or higher "good"
  - 55% of pages are meeting our goal of 75% or higher "good," while 62% of pages are scoring 70% or higher
  - 17% of pages are scoring at less than 50% "good," with 4% scoring at less than 10% "good" 
- We continue to have high numbers of "bad" responses for sign-in-related pages.
- Four pages have consistently gotten the highest volume of total responses and >75% "good" rating over the past 7 months: "What should I bring to my health care appointments," "VA claim exam," The PACT Act and your VA benefits," and "How to check in with your smartphone for some VA appointments"
