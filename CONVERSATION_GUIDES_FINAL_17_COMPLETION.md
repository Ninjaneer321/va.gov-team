## 🎨 Metadata Quality Standards

All frontmatter includes:

### Core Fields
- ✅ **type:** conversation_guide (or template/documentation)
- ✅ **study_name:** Descriptive study title
- ✅ **team:** Team conducting research
- ✅ **product:** Product or feature being studied
- ✅ **platform:** Platform context (CMS, VA.gov, Mobile App, etc.)

### Research Details
- ✅ **moderator:** Lead moderator name
- ✅ **date:** Month and year of research
- ✅ **research_method:** Specific methodology used
- ✅ **session_type:** moderated_remote, moderated_in_person, etc.
- ✅ **session_duration_minutes:** Planned session length
- ✅ **number_of_participants:** Sample size

### Discoverability
- ✅ **tags:** Comprehensive tags including:
  - Product/team area
  - Research methodology
  - Specific features tested
  - User populations
  - Platform components
