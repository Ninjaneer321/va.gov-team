# Top User Pain Points

## My VA

### Pain Point #1
**As a user, I need consistency when moving between My VA and other tools so that it feels like one continuous experience.**

When users click into a tool from My VA, the experience can change in labels, structure, or context, making it feel like a separate system. This forces users to re-orient themselves and interrupts task completion.

*Example:* A user may click from the My VA claims and appeals card into the CST tracker and see different terminology or structure for the same claim than what they just saw.

**Related initiatives:**
- My VA Phase I  
- My VA: Enabling sustainable partner integrations  

---

### Pain Point #2
**As a user, I need My VA to clearly show what matters right now so that I know what I need to do next.**

My VA does not always clearly highlight the most important or time-sensitive actions, leaving users unsure what to focus on. This reduces its effectiveness as a dashboard and can cause users to miss critical tasks.

**Related initiatives:**
- My VA Phase I  
- My VA: Simplifying complex information into actionable summaries  
- My VA: Driving user action/attention  
- My VA: Centralizing Veteran notifications  

---

### Pain Point #3
**As a user, I need My VA to feel relevant to me so that I only see information and actions that apply to my situation.**

The experience can feel too generic, showing content that does not reflect the user’s specific benefits, needs, or life stage. This makes it harder for users to identify what actually matters to them.

*Example:* Users have requested more personalized and customizable content, including the ability to tailor what they see.

**Related initiatives:**
- My VA Phase I  
- My VA: Enabling sustainable partner integrations  

---

## Profile

### Pain Point #4
**As a user, I need to understand where my information is used, and what my updates will affect, so that I can confidently manage my account.**

When users update information in Profile, it’s not clear which benefits or services that change applies to. This creates confusion about whether their information is correct and where else they may need to update it.

*Example:* Updating direct deposit or address information may apply to some benefits, but not others, due to separate systems.

**Related initiatives:**
- Profile: Post 2.0 Improvements  

---

### Pain Point #5
**As a user, I need to understand what information I can update, and what I can’t, so that I know how to keep my information accurate.**

Users expect to be able to edit most of the information shown in Profile, but when they can’t, it’s not always clear why or what to do instead. This creates confusion about ownership and next steps.

**Related initiatives:**
- Profile: Post 2.0 Improvements  

---

### Pain Point #6
**As a user, I need Profile to include the information and features I expect so that I can manage my account in one place.**

Profile does not contain all of the information and functionality Veterans expect, which makes it feel incomplete and limits its usefulness. This creates a fragmented experience and reduces confidence in Profile as a central account management tool.

**Related initiatives:**
- International Phone  
- Profile: Post 2.0 Improvements  
- Benefits Overview  

---

# Bluesky Solutions

## Bluesky #1: Notification-driven UX
Proactively surfaces timely, personalized updates to Veterans (instead of waiting for them to seek information).

**Related initiatives:**
- My VA: Driving user action/attention  
- My VA: Centralizing Veteran notifications  

---

## Bluesky #2: Dashboard
Presents a minimal, scannable view of a Veteran’s most important information and time-sensitive actions (“check engine lights”), which My VA is actively implementing through Phase I cleanup and future alert-driven prioritization.

**Related initiatives:**
- My VA Phase I  
- Benefits Overview  

---

## Bluesky #3: Flattened IA
Reduces reliance on deep navigation by enabling direct, lateral movement to tasks and tools, with My VA and Profile serving as a launch point for faster access to what users need without navigating VA’s complex structure.

**Related initiatives:**
- My VA Phase I  
- Profile: Post 2.0 Improvements  
- My VA: Simplifying complex information into actionable summaries  

---

## Bluesky #4: Standardized data
Establishes a consistent way to structure and surface information across VA systems (via shared patterns and data models), enabling both My VA and Profile to present information in a unified, predictable way.

This supports scalable partner integrations in My VA (e.g., summary cards and direct integrations) while also unifying navigation, content, and linking in Profile so Veterans can more easily find and understand their information across the VA ecosystem.

**Related initiatives:**
- My VA: Enabling sustainable partner integrations  
- My VA: Simplifying complex information into actionable summaries  
- Profile: Post 2.0 Improvements
