# My VA | Forms and applications integration checklist

Changes to the Forms and applications section of My VA require coordination with the Authenticated Experience team. There are current technical limitations as well as governance standards to be aware of when making changes to this section.

If you want to make changes to the Forms and applications section of My VA, you should follow these steps:

- [ ] If you are adding a new form or application, [review this documentation](https://github.com/department-of-veterans-affairs/va.gov-team/blob/master/products/identity-personalization/my-va/forms-status-on-My-VA/adding-a-form.md) to prepare for the development, and [review this documentation](https://github.com/department-of-veterans-affairs/va.gov-team/blob/master/products/identity-personalization/team/governance/UX/my-va-design-content-standards.md#forms-and-applications) to prepare designs;  
- [ ] Submit an intake request to [Authenticated Experience](https://github.com/department-of-veterans-affairs/va.gov-team/issues/new?template=auth-exp-request.yml) so we can review your proposed changes and prioritize coordination with your team;  
- [ ] Meet with the Authenticated Experience UX team to review your designs and discuss content; **Note:** There are currently only 4 statuses supported, and most content is fixed. Anything outside of the templates is a custom request and needs to be discussed with the team for feasibility;  
- [ ] Reach out in \#authexp-engineering with any technical questions before getting started;  
- [ ] Develop behind a feature flag;  
- [ ] Finalize development plans and keep Authenticated Experience advised of when your changes are expected to go live.
