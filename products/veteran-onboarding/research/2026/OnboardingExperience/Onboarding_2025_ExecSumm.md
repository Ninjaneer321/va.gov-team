## Top 5 Pain Points (Why People Fail)

### 1. Verification-first gate blocks momentum
**What's happening:** Users are forced to verify before starting a benefits task, interrupting their flow.  
**Impact:** Many abandon before they can begin an application.  
**Fix:** Remove the global gate; verify only when needed for a chosen action; preserve progress.

### 2. No clear “Apply” path at first login
**What's happening:** The post-login hub lacks a clear Apply path, causing veterans to stall.  
**Impact:** Users fail to start the application they came to complete.  
**Fix:** Add an Apply‑focused first‑login modal and a persistent Getting Started link.

### 3. Dense “Get benefit” pages bury eligibility
**What's happening:** Benefit pages are cluttered and hide eligibility details.  
**Impact:** Users can’t confirm whether they qualify and often quit.  
**Fix:** Convert pages to an About/How‑to layout, surface eligibility early, and simplify links.

### 4. Task list is hard to find and unclear
**What's happening:** The task list has low visibility and doesn’t clearly show completion.  
**Impact:** Users don’t finish key onboarding steps or trust that their updates saved.  
**Fix:** Make it prominent, rename for clarity, and add explicit save/complete signals.

### 5. Guidance isn’t personalized
**What's happening:** The experience presents generic guidance instead of using known data.  
**Impact:** Veterans miss relevant benefits and spend extra time re-entering information.  
**Fix:** Provide personalized outcomes and pre‑populate forms using profile data.
