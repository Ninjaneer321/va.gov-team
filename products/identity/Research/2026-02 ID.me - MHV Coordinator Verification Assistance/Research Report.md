# Summary

Coordinators across all five sites are handling a significant daily burden helping Veterans navigate ID.me identity verification and troubleshooting processes. Each coordinator supports 2 to 12+ Veterans per day, with each case taking 20 to 60+ minutes. More than half relate directly to ID.me. Many issues fall outside coordinators’ control or permissions.

Common issues include:
- Personal email access problems
- MFA/phone carrier failures
- Technical problems with devices
- Escalation wait times
- Inconsistent ID.me flows
- Policy gaps for caregivers or POA

Coordinators have developed workarounds (video verification, checklists, pre‑screening, coaching), but these are not standardized and rely on individual effort.

# Recurring Failures Drive Most of the Support Burden

## 1. Existing ID.me Accounts Veterans Cannot Access
Forgotten passwords, inaccessible emails, MFA tied to old phones.

> “They go in and say, ‘I already have an ID.me account… when did this happen?’” – C4

## 2. Account Recovery Barriers
Email delays, inbox limits, reset-loop caused by ID.me’s two-email flow.

## 3. Phone & MFA Carrier Incompatibility
Prepaid and VoIP carriers often unsupported.

> “If they say Consumer Cellular, Boost, Cricket, Jitterbug… we don’t even try.” – C2

## 4. Document Capture & Device Barriers
Older devices, camera permissions, poor lighting, difficulty with selfies.

## 5. Escalation Pathway Burdens
Long help desk waits (30–40+ mins), chat timeouts, inconsistent chatbot.

## 6. Inconsistent ID.me Flows
Unexpected changes in document options, recovery code prompts, UPS availability.

## 7. Veteran Resistance to Video Verification
Privacy concerns, distrust, refusal.

## 8. No Caregiver or POA Pathway
Homebound Veterans and those with cognitive impairment lack accessible options.


# Next Steps (Draft)

### Connect ID.me With VA Identity Engineering
Discuss ways to reduce repeated sign-ins.

### Improve In-Flow Messaging
Clarify selfie consent, document requirements.

### Technical Investigations
- Reauthorization prompts (~every 10 logins)
- Multiple password-reset emails
- UPS option inconsistencies

## Focus Area 1: Fix Password & Email Loop
- Consolidate reset to one email
- Warn against browser-generated passwords
- Improve email deliverability and error handling

## Focus Area 2: Reduce Lockouts
Remove unnecessary reauthentication; improve error messages.

## Focus Area 3: Improve Flow Consistency
Fix post-photo stall, standardize UI, ensure recovery codes display.

## Focus Area 4: Standardize Coordinator Preparation
Adopt a national checklist; standardize MPI usage; create update cadence.
