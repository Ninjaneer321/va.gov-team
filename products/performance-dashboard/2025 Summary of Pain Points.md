# Performance Dashboard – Structured Findings: Web Overview (GA Analytics 2025)


## Executive Summary

**Traffic volume contracted materially across 2025, with sessions falling from a March peak (3,737) to an October trough (2,571), a drop of ~31%.**
**Engagement quality improved late‑year—average session time reached 50s in November—yet was volatile, dipping sharply to 43s in December.**
**Overall depth remained shallow at ~1.60–1.73 pages per session, suggesting limited content exploration and potential navigational friction.**
**Primary drivers appear to be acquisition slumps, intermittent engagement dips, and seasonal spikes that mask underlying weaknesses.**

CSAT is **not available** in GA; this report uses engagement and volume proxies to pinpoint pain points.
**P0 recommendations** focus on restoring acquisition, increasing content depth, and stabilizing engagement.

# 4. Category 1: Acquisition Decline (Traffic Drop)

Traffic fell sharply after March's peak and bottomed in October.

### Representative Metrics
- Mar → Apr: **3,737 → 3,213 (−14.0%)**
- Apr → May: **3,213 → 2,869 (−10.7%)**
- Jul → Aug: **3,196 → 2,867 (−10.3%)**
- Sep → Oct: **2,819 → 2,571 (−8.8%)**

### Impact
Shrinking reach reduces total exposure to dashboard content and amplifies the effect of engagement dips.

### Recommendations
- **P0:** Always‑on acquisition (SEO/SEM + referral partners).
- **P1:** Content syndication & SEO refresh.
- **P1:** Quarterly campaigns to avoid post‑peak drop‑off.



# 5. Category 2: Engagement Dip (Short Session Duration)

Engagement fluctuated significantly across 2025.

### Representative Metrics
- Feb → Mar: **43s → 40s**
- May → Jun: **45s → 41s**
- Nov → Dec: **50s → 43s**

### Impact
Low content depth combined with short sessions reduces task completion and value extraction.

### Recommendations
- **P0:** Improve landing structure & above‑fold density.
- **P1:** Add “next best view” recommendations.
- **P2:** Run A/B tests during high‑reach periods.



# 6. Category 3: Shallow Content Depth (Low Pages/Session)

Pages per session remained low all year.

- **High:** June (~1.73)
- **Low:** September (~1.60)

### Impact
Users are viewing 1–2 pages before exiting, limiting value discovery.

### Recommendations
- **P0:** Add contextual/related content modules.
- **P1:** Use micro‑copy to highlight advanced sections.
- **P2:** Publish weekly “insights digest” to deepen recurring visits.



# 7. Category 4: Seasonality Dependence

Key spikes:
- **March:** Traffic peak
- **November:** Engagement peak

Seasonality masks underlying retention/acquisition issues.

### Recommendations
- **P1:** Staggered micro‑campaigns.
- **P2:** Align product announcements with optimized landing flows.
