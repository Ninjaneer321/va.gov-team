# **VA.gov – Structured Findings: 1095-B Tax Form Access & Download Flow (Call-Center Insights)**


## **Executive Summary**

Across **7 call-center cases (Mar 6–Dec 15, 2025)**, callers primarily sought help to **access, download, or receive VA Form 1095-B** for tax filing. **No CSAT values were provided** in this dataset; as a proxy, **call resolution was 85.7% “Closed” (6/7) with 14.3% “Routed” (1/7)**, indicating most issues were solvable at first contact but access barriers still triggered escalations.\
The **top pain points** were **authentication/login & MFA** (3/7; **43%**), **navigation/discovery gaps** (2/7; **29%**), **browser/technical compatibility** (1/7; **14%**), and **channel/policy clarity**—including **availability timing (February)** and non-digital options (1/7; **14%**).\
Drivers included **inability to log in**, **MFA resets via ID.me**, **needing explicit “Download 1095-B” paths**, and **uncertainty about when/where the form is available without using a computer**. **Data Range: Call-center transcripts from Mar 6, 2025 to Dec 15, 2025 (n=7).**

## **1. Top-Level Findings (Ranked by Relevance & Criticality)**

1. **Authentication & Account Access** — **3/7 (42.9%)**: Callers could not log in, needed **MFA resets**, or hadn’t used the portal recently, blocking self-service access to 1095-B. 

2. **Navigation & Discovery** — **2/7 (28.6%)**: Callers needed guidance to find the tax forms (e.g., QuickSubmit, locating the 1095-B download). 

3. **Technical Compatibility (Browser)** — **1/7 (14.3%)**: A caller could only access the needed site after switching from **Edge to Chrome**. 

4. **Channel & Policy Clarity** — **1/7 (14.3%)**: Confusion around **form availability (February timing)** and non-digital alternatives for veterans who **don’t use a computer**.

## **4. Category 1: Authentication & Account Access**

**Mentions / Volume:** **3/7 (42.9%)**. 

**Description:** Veterans experienced **sign-in failures**, **MFA complications**, or **lapsed portal access**, blocking self-service retrieval of 1095-B and requiring agent intervention or **routing to another line**. 

**Representative quotes or metrics:**

- “**Veteran is calling in to receive tax form documents. Veteran states he is unable to log in to VA.gov.**” 

- “**He is having issues with log in … needs help … reset MFA in ID ME … needed his 1095 B for taxes.**” 

- “**Needs his tax forms online but he has not logged into his portal in a while … provided NCC number and warm transferred.**” (**status: Routed**) 

**Impact:** Authentication barriers directly **block task completion**, forcing manual support, transfers, and delayed access to tax forms—especially critical during early tax-filing season. **One case required routing rather than resolution at first contact**, indicating a risk of longer time-to-document. 

**Temporal or segment patterns (if applicable):**

| Month          | Count (Auth)   |
| -------------- | -------------- |
| **March 2025** | **1**          |
| **April 2025** | **1**          |
| **July 2025**  | **1** (Routed) |

**Critical Observations:**

- **Authentication pain peaked around early tax activity** (Mar–Apr), with **persistent needs into July** for portal re-access. 

- **MFA resets via ID.me** were necessary but **did not directly surface a fast path to 1095-B**, causing extra steps and calls. 

- **Routing in July** suggests **non-self-service fallback** (email/phone) remains important when portal access is not recoverable quickly. 

**Recommendations:**

- **Add a pre-sign-in “1095-B” call-to-action and post-sign-in deep link** on the authentication success page to reduce search time after MFA reset. 

- **Streamline MFA recovery** with a **tax-season banner** (“Reset MFA → Download your 1095-B”) and contextual help. 

- **Offer short-term bypass**: if identity is verified via call center, allow **secure email delivery** or **mail request capture** without forcing same-session portal re-entry.

## **5. Category 2: Navigation & Discovery**

**Mentions / Volume:** **2/7 (28.6%)**. 

**Description:** Callers struggled to **find where to upload or download tax forms**, requiring **agent guidance** (e.g., **QuickSubmit**, walking through the **1095-B download** steps) and additional information like **pharmacy spend totals**. 

**Representative quotes or metrics:**

- “**The Veteran wanted to upload his tax documents. Guided the Veteran on using QuickSubmit.**” 

- “**Asked for assistance downloading his 1095-B and getting a list of how much he spent on medication last year … agent walked him through downloading 1095-B.**” 

**Impact:** **Discovery gaps** increase **call volume** and time-to-task, especially when callers expect one obvious **“Download 1095-B”** path and related tax-season resources (e.g., spend statements). 

**Temporal or segment patterns (if applicable):**

| Month          | Count (Navigation) |
| -------------- | ------------------ |
| **March 2025** | **2**              |

**Critical Observations:**

- **Navigation issues spiked in March**, aligning with tax prep activities. 

- Callers also wanted **adjacent tax artifacts** (e.g., **pharmacy spend totals**), implying the need for a consolidated **Tax Documents Hub**. 

- Effective **agent walkthroughs resolved cases**, but this indicates **self-service discoverability gaps**. 

**Recommendations:**

- Create a **prominent “1095-B Tax Documents” landing page** with a **one-click Download** and **FAQ** (availability, data sources, related statements). 

- Add **guided “Where to find my tax forms”** microcopy and **contextual inline links** from relevant account and benefits pages. 

- Integrate **pharmacy spend statements** or cross-link to the appropriate **support line** within the same hub.

## **6. Category 3: Technical Compatibility (Browser)**

**Mentions / Volume:** **1/7 (14.3%)**. 

**Description:** One caller **could not access** the needed tax site until **switching from Microsoft Edge to Google Chrome**, implying **compatibility or configuration friction**. 

**Representative quotes or metrics:**

- “**Agent was able to help the veteran with changing to Chrome … able to gain access after he changed from Edge to Chrome.**” 

**Impact:** Subtle **browser issues** can produce **hard blocks** at the point of access, eroding trust and pushing users to **higher-cost channels** (calls, transfers). 

**Temporal or segment patterns (if applicable):**

| Month          | Count (Tech) |
| -------------- | ------------ |
| **March 2025** | **1**        |

**Critical Observations:**

- Even with low volume, **browser incompatibility** is **task-blocking** and highly frustrating during tax season. 

- When users switch browsers mid-call, they often **complete successfully**, suggesting **detectable symptoms** amenable to preemptive UX cues. 

**Recommendations:**

- Add **client-side checks** and **browser guidance** (“If you experience issues, try Chrome or update your browser”) near the **1095-B entry points**. 

- Publish a **compatibility matrix** with clear troubleshooting steps and **auto-detect messaging** for known edge cases.

***

## **7. Category 4: Channel & Policy Clarity (Availability & Offline Options)**

**Mentions / Volume:** **1/7 (14.3%)**. 

**Description:** A veteran who **does not use a computer** was told the **1095-B** could **not be obtained until February 1 by mail**, prompting questions about **why the form isn’t available earlier** and how to get it without digital access. 

**Representative quotes or metrics:**

- “**Veteran … was told that she could not get the tax form … until after February 1 by mail … usually files early and needs her 1095-B.**” 

**Impact:** **Unclear timing** and **offline pathways** cause **frustration** for early filers and non-digital users, leading to **repeat contacts** and potential delays in filing. 

**Temporal or segment patterns (if applicable):**

| Month             | Count (Channel/Policy) |
| ----------------- | ---------------------- |
| **December 2025** | **1**                  |

**Critical Observations:**

- **Pre-February confusion** surfaces in **December**, as early filers begin preparations. 

- **Clear, channel-agnostic guidance** (mail, phone, secure email) is essential for veterans with **limited digital access**. 

**Recommendations:**

- Publish a **tax-season availability notice** (dates, processing timelines) on the **1095-B hub**, including **offline request options**. 

- Enable **single-step mail request capture** via call center with **status tracking** (confirmation and expected arrival).
